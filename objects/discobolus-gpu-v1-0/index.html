Discobolus GPUV1.0

Marble, growing gold, muted blue studio. Fully immersive VR, no passthrough.

Starting native WebGPU…

In VR: walk around, or move with the left stick; right stick snap turns. Right trigger grows gold; left trigger erases. Side grips manipulate the sculpture with Zelda controls; while gripping, sticks control the object. Desktop: left drag grows, Shift + left drag erases; right drag orbits.

Original sculpture assets and reaction-diffusion technique from Marco Fugaro's Discobolus demo. MIT code licence. VR lighting adaptation.
