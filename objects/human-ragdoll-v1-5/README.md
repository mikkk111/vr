# Human Ragdoll V1.5 — compliance, gravity and contact

Preserves V1.4's Jolt WASM 15-body / 14-joint architecture, original GLB, GPU skeletal skinning and shared Zelda controller engine. No new soft body solver or impulses from the regional springs into the skeleton.

Actual rigid-body masses normalised from 69.6 kg to 60 kg, with the previous relative mass distribution retained. Per-controller force now capped at 230 N / handling-weight setting; gravity remains 9.81 m/s² at unit scale. A local acceleration-limited translation and damped orientation follower sits between the shared grip target and visible body, giving whole-object motion inertia. This is simulated heft, not actual force feedback. The follower settles to the fixed target after release. Higher handling-weight settings reduce controller force and anchor acceleration; they do not change physical mass.

Four independent bounded regional springs, separate firmness/damping and gain for chest/gluteal regions, gravity equilibrium, and velocity-driven local contact from both free controllers. Grip-space pose is sampled in the XR animation frame; controller centre is offset by 45 mm along its forward direction and the contact radius is 45 mm at unit scale. Original model vertex samples provide local contact surface estimates; no remote ray contact. Squeeze/trigger pressed excludes contact, to avoid interfering with manipulation. Swept crossing, pressure, tangential speed, capped impulse and cooldown avoid repeated impacts from a stationary controller. Contact approximation is not full triangle collision and no hardware haptics are claimed. Contacts counter reports active region/controller pairs.

Default settings: chest gain 1.25, gluteal gain 1.15; firmness .55/.60; damping .34/.38; sag 1.15; contact 1.4; heft 1; floor restitution .12. Range of gain/contact/sag 0–3, firmness .35–1.60, damping .15–1.40, heft .25–2.50. Presets Natural/soft, Subtle, Responsive, Off.

Expanded hull planes tested against original 2,077 candidate vertices (2,002 active): max signed residual -0.20 mm under simultaneous independent bounded offsets; minimum nonadjacent rest separating-face gap 0.0772 mm. Maximum actual local vertical vertex offsets relative to bind geometry ~40.8 mm chest and 35.6 mm gluteal; not a guarantee of separation in articulated poses. All 673 contact surface samples derived from original mesh. Default upright spring-handle static sag 26.05/12.55 mm, before mask weights; synthetic impulse test recovered to equilibrium. Maximum-setting tests stayed within displacement caps and recovered within 0.07 mm in six seconds. ContactDrive entry/hold/exit regression passed, with no repeated impulse on stationary hold. JavaScript parsed. Browser, native engine drop, headset input and FPS not independently verified.
