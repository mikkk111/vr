Dual-hand tabletop physics · Quest 3 passthrough

Gravity always on · higher friction and damping · heavier-feeling, less slippery noodles.

Point both controllers at strands and hold both triggers to manipulate two particles independently. Release either trigger to drop that strand. Side grips move and resize the table and noodles together using Zelda v13 controls. Release the trigger to drop a strand onto the table. Desktop: left mouse drags a noodle, right mouse orbits.

Original solver by Marco Fugaro · MIT licence · Original demo. VR adaptation with a visible wooden table, tabletop collisions and no rendered floor.
